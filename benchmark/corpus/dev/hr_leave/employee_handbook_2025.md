# Employee Handbook (2025 edition)

## Working at Halden
Standard working time is 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are agreed within each team.

## Probation
New employees complete a probation period of 6 months. Objectives are agreed with the manager in the first two weeks.

## Time off
All employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave each year. Paid sick leave is 10 days per year. Please inform your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop and a headset on their first day.
