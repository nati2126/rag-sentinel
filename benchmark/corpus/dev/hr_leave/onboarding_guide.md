# Welcome to Halden Systems

## Your first week
Your laptop is delivered on day one and your IT account is activated before you arrive. You must complete the mandatory security awareness training within 14 days of your start date.

## Benefits at a glance
You start with 20 days of annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Health insurance starts on the first day of the month after you join.

## Who to ask
Your onboarding buddy is your first point of contact for practical questions.
