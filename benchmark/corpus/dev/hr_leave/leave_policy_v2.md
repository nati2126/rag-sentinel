# Annual Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how paid annual leave is earned, requested and recorded at Halden Systems. Version 2.0 replaces version 1.0.

## 2. Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours. Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave.

## 3. Long-service days
Employees with five or more years of continuous service receive 2 additional days of annual leave per year.

## 4. Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire.

## 5. Requesting leave
Requests for more than three consecutive days must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Managers respond to leave requests within five working days.
