# Sick Leave Policy

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days of sickness absence. Unused sick leave cannot be carried over or paid out.

Employees must notify their manager before the start of their working day when they are unable to work.
