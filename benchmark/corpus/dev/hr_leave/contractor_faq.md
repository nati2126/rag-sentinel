# Contractor FAQ

**Are contractors entitled to paid annual leave?**
No. Contractors are not eligible for paid annual leave. Contractors may take up to 10 unpaid days off per year, agreed in advance with their project lead.

**How do contractors invoice?**
Contractors invoice monthly. Invoices are paid within 30 days of receipt.
