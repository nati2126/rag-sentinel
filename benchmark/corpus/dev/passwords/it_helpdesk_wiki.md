# Accounts and passwords (helpdesk notes)

Reset your password at the self-service portal. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and changed every 90 days.

If your account is locked, call the helpdesk on extension 4400. Helpdesk hours are 08:00 to 18:00 on working days.
