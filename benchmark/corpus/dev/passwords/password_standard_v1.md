# Password Standard

## Scope
Applies to all Halden Systems accounts, including employees and contractors.

## Requirements
Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and include an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a symbol. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts.

## Storage
Passwords must never be written down or shared.
