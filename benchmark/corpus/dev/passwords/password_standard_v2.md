# Password Standard

## Scope
Applies to all Halden Systems accounts, including employees and contractors.

## Requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Composition rules (mandatory symbols or numbers) no longer apply; passphrases are encouraged. Passwords are no longer changed on a fixed schedule; a password must be changed only when compromise is suspected. Accounts are locked after 10 failed login attempts.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all remote access.

## Storage
Passwords must never be written down or shared. Use the company password manager.
