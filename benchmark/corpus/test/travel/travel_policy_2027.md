# Travel and Expenses Policy (2027) - approved, effective January 1, 2027

## Meals
The domestic meal per diem is $70 per travel day.

## Accommodation
Hotel costs are reimbursed up to $200 per night for domestic travel.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip, with itemised receipts.
