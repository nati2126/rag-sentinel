# Expense Policy

## Submitting claims
Expense claims are submitted in ExpenseHub. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

## Receipts
A receipt must be attached for every expense above $25.

## Mileage
Business use of a private car is reimbursed at $0.45 per kilometre.
