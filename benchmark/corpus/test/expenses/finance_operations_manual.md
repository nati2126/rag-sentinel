# Finance Operations Manual

## Expense processing
Finance Operations reviews expense claims every Friday. A receipt must be attached for every expense above $75.
Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date; later claims are rejected.

## Payments
Approved expense claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.
