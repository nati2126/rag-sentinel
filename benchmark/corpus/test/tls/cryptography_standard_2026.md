# Cryptography Standard

This standard replaces the 2019 Transport Encryption Standard.

## Transport encryption
The minimum TLS version for all services, internal and public, is TLS 1.2. TLS 1.3 is preferred for new services. TLS 1.0 and TLS 1.1 are prohibited, in line with RFC 8996.

## Certificates
Certificates are renewed at least 30 days before they expire. Certificate keys must be at least 2048 bits for RSA.
