# Customer Support Service Levels

| Priority | First response |
|---|---|
| P1 (service down) | 1 hour |
| P2 (major degradation) | 4 hours |
| P3 (minor issue) | 2 business days |

P1 issues receive a first response within 1 hour, 24 hours a day. P2 issues receive a first response within 4 hours. P3 issues receive a first response within 2 business days.
