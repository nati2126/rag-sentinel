# Security Incident Response Policy

## Reporting
Employees must report suspected security incidents to the security team within 24 hours of discovery, using the incident form or the security hotline.

## Severity
Severity 1 incidents require status updates to the incident channel every 2 hours until they are contained.

## Review
A post-incident review is completed for every severity 1 and severity 2 incident.
