# Security Operations Manual

## Purpose
This manual describes how the corporate security team protects people, offices and equipment. It covers
reception procedures, alarm handling, camera surveillance, key management and incident escalation for
all Halden Systems offices. Security staff follow it on every shift, and changes are agreed with the
facilities team before they take effect.

## Reception and visitors
Visitors must be registered at least 48 hours in advance so that reception can prepare a badge.
Reception checks photo identification for every visitor on arrival.

## Camera surveillance
CCTV footage is retained for 30 days and then deleted automatically, unless it is needed for an
investigation.

## Keys and alarms
Physical keys are issued only to facilities staff. Alarm codes are changed every 6 months.
