# Mobile Device Policy

## Scope
This policy covers company-issued mobile phones and tablets used by employees. It sets out how devices
are issued, used, protected and replaced. It does not cover personal devices; those are addressed in
separate guidance when they are used for work. Managers request devices for new team members through
the IT service portal.

## Replacement
Company phones are replaced every 3 years, or earlier if they are damaged beyond repair.

## Data and roaming
Each company phone has a monthly mobile data allowance of 20 GB. International roaming must be
activated by the IT service desk before travel.

## Personal use
Personal use of company phones is allowed if it is reasonable and does not interfere with work.
