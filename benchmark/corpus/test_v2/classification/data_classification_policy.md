# Data Classification Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how information is classified so that it is protected according to its sensitivity.
It applies to all information created, received or stored by Halden Systems, in any format, including
documents, databases, e-mail, chat messages and paper records. Every information owner is responsible
for classifying the information they create and for keeping the classification up to date.

## Classification levels
Halden Systems uses four classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted.
Public information may be shared with anyone. Internal information is for employees and contractors.
Confidential information is limited to the people who need it for their work. Restricted information
is the most sensitive category and includes personal data of customers and security keys.

## Handling
Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Confidential data must be stored only in
approved company systems. Information without a label is treated as Internal until it is classified.

## Review
Confidential documents must be reviewed for classification every 24 months. Owners record the review
date in the document properties.
