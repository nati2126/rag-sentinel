# Records Management Standard

## Scope
This standard applies to business records in all formats.

## Classification review
Confidential documents must be reviewed for classification every 12 months.

## Disposal
Paper records are destroyed with cross-cut shredders. Electronic records are deleted using the approved
secure deletion tool.
