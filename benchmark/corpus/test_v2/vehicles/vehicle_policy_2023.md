# Company Vehicle Policy

## Replacement
Company cars are replaced after 5 years or 150,000 km, whichever comes first.

## Accidents
Drivers must report accidents within 48 hours.

## Fuel
Fuel cards may be used for business fuel only. Petrol, diesel, hybrid and electric cars may be ordered.
