# On-Call Policy

## Allowance
Engineers on the on-call rota receive an on-call allowance of $250 per week.

## Call-outs
Time spent handling a call-out is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate.
After a call-out between 00:00 and 06:00, engineers are entitled to a minimum rest period of 11 hours.

## Rota
An engineer is on call at most 1 week in 4.
