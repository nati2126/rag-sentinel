# Engineering Handbook

## Being on call
On-call engineers receive $250 per week for being on the rota. Acknowledge pages within 15 minutes.
Your on-call laptop must have the VPN client installed and tested before your shift.

## Code review
Every change needs at least one approving review before it is merged.
