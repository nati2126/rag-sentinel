# Access Control Standard

## Access reviews
Privileged access is reviewed every 3 months. Standard user access is reviewed every 6 months.

## Leavers
Accounts of leavers are disabled within 24 hours of the termination date.

## Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for all administrator accounts.
