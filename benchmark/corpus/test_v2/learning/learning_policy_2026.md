# Learning and Development Policy

## Budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of $1,500.

## Study leave
Employees may take 3 days of paid study leave per year.

## Certifications
Certification exam fees are reimbursed after the employee passes the exam.
Attending an external conference requires manager approval.
