# Remote Work Policy (draft for consultation)

## Remote days
Employees may work remotely up to 4 days per week. Teams agree one shared office day.

## Core hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

## Home office
A one-time home office stipend of $500 is available for desk, chair or monitor purchases.
