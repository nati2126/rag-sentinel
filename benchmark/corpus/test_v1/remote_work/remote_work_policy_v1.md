# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All employees who have completed probation may work remotely, subject to manager agreement.

## Remote days
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Remote days are agreed with the manager in advance and recorded in the team calendar.

## Core hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Employees must be reachable during core hours on remote days.

## Home office
A one-time home office stipend of $300 is available for desk, chair or monitor purchases.
