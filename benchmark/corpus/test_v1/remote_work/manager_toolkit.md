# Manager Toolkit: leading hybrid teams

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week; you approve remote days in advance. Hold a team meeting at least once a week with cameras on.

One-to-one meetings should take place every two weeks. Use the team calendar to plan office days so that people overlap.
