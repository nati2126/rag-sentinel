# Parental Leave Policy

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.

Employees become eligible after 6 months of employment. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
