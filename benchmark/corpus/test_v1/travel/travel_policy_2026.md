# Travel and Expenses Policy (2026)

## Meals
The domestic meal per diem is $60 per travel day.

## Accommodation
Hotel costs are reimbursed up to $200 per night for domestic travel.

## Flights
Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 8 hours.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip, with itemised receipts. Claims without receipts are rejected.
