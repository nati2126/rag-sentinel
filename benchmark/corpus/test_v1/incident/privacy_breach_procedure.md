# Personal Data Breach Procedure

When a security incident involves personal data, the Data Protection Office assesses the risk to individuals.

A personal data breach must be notified to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by GDPR Article 33. Affected individuals are informed without undue delay when the breach is likely to result in a high risk to them.
