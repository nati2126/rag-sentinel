Hardware Lifecycle Policy

This policy replaces the 2023 IT Asset Standard.

Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Monitors are replaced every 6 years. Mobile phones are replaced every 3 years.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours. Retired devices are wiped and recycled through the approved recycling partner.
