Speak-Up Policy

Reporting. Anyone can report a suspected breach of law or of our code of conduct. Reports can be made
anonymously through the speak-up hotline.

Timelines. Reports are acknowledged within 7 days. The reporter receives feedback within 3 months.

Protection. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.
