Company Vehicle Policy 2026

Replacement. Company cars are replaced after 4 years or 120,000 km, whichever comes first.

New cars. Only electric or hybrid cars may be ordered as new company cars.

Accidents. Drivers must report accidents within 24 hours.

Private use. Company cars may be used privately for up to 5,000 km per year.

Fuel. Fuel cards may be used for business fuel only.
